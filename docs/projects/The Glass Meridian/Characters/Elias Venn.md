# Character Summary

Elias Venn helped integrate older municipal sensors into Meridian. He discovered discrepancies between raw flood-model uncertainty and the public risk layer.

## Canonical Identity

## Core Character Concept

A systems engineer leaves physical clues because he no longer trusts the digital system he helped build.

## Family & Background

Elias keeps his private life separate from work and has no immediate family in Lumen.

## Education & Career

Trained in civil systems engineering and hydrologic telemetry. Joined Helix during Lumen's post-Nightwater modernization.

## Important Life Events

- Integrated legacy tide gauges into Meridian.
- Discovered a mismatch between uncertainty data and public classifications.
- Began a private audit.
- Gave the modified survey compass to Tomas.
- Disappeared shortly before the story opens.

## Key Relationships

**Tomas Vale:** Technical contact trusted with the compass.

**Mara Vale:** Municipal mapping colleague whose attention to base-layer integrity made her a possible ally.

## Professional & Practical Strengths

Hydrologic telemetry, network architecture, sensor integration, and system auditing.

## Personality

### Strengths

Meticulous, patient, technically creative.

### Flaws

Conflict-avoidant, secretive, slow to ask for help.

### Contradictions

Builds transparent systems but responds to danger by hiding his own trail.

## Psychological & Behavioral Habits

Leaves written checklists beside physical equipment even when the same records exist digitally.

## Life Outside the Main Story

Repairs antique optical instruments.

## Character Arc & Progression

### Stage One

Discovers the classification discrepancy.

### Stage Two

Attempts internal escalation.

### Stage Three

Concludes the audit trail may be altered.

### Stage Four

Moves evidence into physical locations.

### Stage Five

His disappearance forces others to complete the investigation.

## Voice & Dialogue Notes

Quiet, dryly humorous, and specific. Avoids dramatic language.

## Continuity Notes

Elias is alive during the opening chapters. His location remains unknown in the initial demo material.
