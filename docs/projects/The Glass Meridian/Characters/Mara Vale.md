# Character Summary

Mara Vale is a precise, observant municipal cartographer who believes good information allows people to make better decisions. She has built her identity around accuracy, restraint, and professional neutrality.

## Core Character Concept

A woman who believes maps should tell the truth discovers that the truth has been deliberately removed from the map.

## Family & Background

Mara and her younger brother Tomas were raised in East Reach by their mother, Lena Vale, a transit dispatcher. During the Nightwater Flood, Mara was away at university while Tomas and Lena were trapped in East Reach for hours. Mara's absence contributed to a long-standing guilt she rarely discusses.

## Education & Career

Mara studied geography, GIS, and municipal planning. She joined the city mapping office after graduate school and moved into the Office of Civic Resilience when Meridian consolidated emergency mapping and flood forecasting.

## Important Life Events

- Left East Reach for university.
- Nightwater occurred during her final year.
- Returned to Lumen after graduation.
- Joined municipal mapping.
- Helped migrate historical survey records into Meridian.
- Became increasingly distant from Tomas as their attitudes toward city institutions diverged.

## Key Relationships

**Tomas Vale:** Younger brother. Their affection survives beneath years of frustration.

**Nia Corren:** Investigative journalist and former university acquaintance.

**Elias Venn:** Missing infrastructure engineer Mara knew professionally.

## Professional & Practical Strengths

- Exceptional spatial reasoning.
- Strong GIS and archival-map skills.
- Notices inconsistencies others dismiss as clerical noise.
- Patient researcher.
- Familiar with municipal workflows and bureaucratic records.

## Personality

### Strengths

Methodical, loyal, fair-minded, patient, difficult to intimidate.

### Flaws

Overvalues procedural legitimacy, slow to act without certainty, emotionally guarded.

### Contradictions

Mara distrusts dramatic claims but is drawn to anomalies. She values transparency yet hides her own uncertainty.

## Psychological & Behavioral Habits

- Straightens objects while thinking.
- Marks paper maps in pencil even when digital tools are available.
- Counts intersections when walking unfamiliar routes.
- Avoids saying "I think" when she can substitute a measurable fact.

## Life Outside the Main Story

Lives alone above a closed watch-repair shop near Old Quay. Restores damaged paper maps as a private hobby.

## Character Arc & Progression

### Stage One

Trusts accurate systems and assumes manipulation is exceptional.

### Stage Two

Finds evidence that official maps are selectively incomplete.

### Stage Three

Accepts that technical accuracy can still serve biased priorities.

### Stage Four

Acts before she has perfect certainty because delay carries consequences.

### Stage Five

Reframes professional integrity as accountability to people, not merely procedure.

## Voice & Dialogue Notes

Mara speaks precisely and often corrects vague spatial language. Under stress her sentences shorten. She rarely exaggerates.

## Continuity Notes

Mara is 36 at story opening, was 24 during Nightwater, and initially does not know why Elias disappeared.
