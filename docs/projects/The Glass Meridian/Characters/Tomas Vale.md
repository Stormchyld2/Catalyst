# Character Summary

Tomas repairs navigation equipment, tide sensors, and marine electronics throughout Lumen's harbor. He distrusts Meridian because he sees the failing physical sensors and improvised repairs hidden behind its polished interface.

## Core Character Concept

A hands-on technician who trusts equipment only after opening the casing.

## Family & Background

Tomas remained in East Reach through Nightwater and spent nearly nine hours helping neighbors reach an elevated tram platform.

## Education & Career

He trained in industrial electronics rather than attending university and works on municipal and private marine systems.

## Important Life Events

- Survived Nightwater in East Reach.
- Became certified in marine electronics.
- Refused a permanent Helix maintenance position.
- Received Elias Venn's glass compass two days before Elias disappeared.

## Key Relationships

**Mara Vale:** Older sister. Tomas thinks Mara trusts institutions too easily.

**Elias Venn:** Occasional client and technical contact.

## Professional & Practical Strengths

Electrical troubleshooting, sensor calibration, harbor access, and physical improvisation.

## Personality

### Strengths

Resourceful, direct, protective, funny under pressure.

### Flaws

Suspicious, impatient, holds grudges.

### Contradictions

Distrusts institutions but repeatedly accepts work maintaining the infrastructure they depend on.

## Psychological & Behavioral Habits

Taps tool housings with one knuckle before opening them and keeps stripped screws sorted in bottle caps.

## Life Outside the Main Story

Runs a cramped repair shop behind an old chandlery in East Reach.

## Character Arc & Progression

### Stage One

Assumes Mara will defend Meridian.

### Stage Two

Sees Mara take the anomaly seriously.

### Stage Three

Allows her into Elias's trail.

### Stage Four

Accepts that exposing a system requires preserving what still works.

### Stage Five

Begins rebuilding trust with Mara.

## Voice & Dialogue Notes

Informal and concrete. Uses mechanical analogies and interrupts abstract explanations.

## Continuity Notes

Tomas possesses the compass before Chapter One but does not initially know its function.
