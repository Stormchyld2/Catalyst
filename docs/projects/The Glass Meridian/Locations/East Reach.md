# Location Summary

East Reach is a dense working-class district built along reclaimed tidal flats. It contains Bellwether Row and several neighborhoods identified for long-term managed retreat.

## Physical Description

Row houses, elevated utility conduits, small workshops, concrete flood gates, mural-covered retaining walls.

## Geography & Layout

Low elevation, old drainage channels, and limited high-ground access. The elevated Green Line tram forms one of the few continuous evacuation routes.

## Atmosphere & Sensory Details

Brackish water after rain, compressor hum, tram bells, wet concrete, cooking smells from ground-floor kitchens.

## History

East Reach suffered the highest death toll during Nightwater.

## Culture & Population

Multi-generational families, marine trades, municipal workers, small manufacturers, and strong neighborhood associations.

## Important Areas

Bellwether Row, Tomas's workshop, Green Line viaduct, East Gate flood barrier.

## Connected Characters

Tomas Vale lives and works here. Mara and Tomas grew up here.

## Connected Organizations

The district is heavily affected by Lumen resilience policy and Helix risk classifications.

## Story Function

Makes the consequences of mapping policy visible and personal.

## Events & Scenes

Mara visits Bellwether Row in Chapter One.

## Continuity Notes

Bellwether Row physically exists even when missing from the current public Meridian map.
