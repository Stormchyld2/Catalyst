# Location Summary

A hardened civic facility on high ground combining emergency management, mapping, sensor telemetry, and public communications.

## Canonical Identity

| Field | Canon |

## Physical Description

Low concrete structure, narrow storm-rated windows, layered security doors, large wall displays, and redundant power systems.

## Geography & Layout

Mapping bay and forecast floor occupy the central level. Server and telemetry rooms sit below grade. Executive emergency coordination rooms overlook the main operations wall.

## Atmosphere & Sensory Details

Filtered air, quiet fans, muted alarms, bright map displays, coffee, and the vibration of backup generators during storms.

## History

Built after Nightwater as part of Lumen's resilience reconstruction.

## Culture & Population

Civil servants and Helix contractors work side by side, creating blurred lines of responsibility.

## Important Areas

Mapping bay, forecast wall, server corridor, emergency coordination room.

## Connected Characters

Mara works here. Elias had technical access.

## Connected Organizations

Lumen Civic Government and Helix Municipal Systems.

## Story Function

Represents the polished institutional face of a system whose inputs are more uncertain than its displays suggest.

## Events & Scenes

Opening scene and late-story emergency operations.

## Continuity Notes

The facility is on high ground and is designed to remain operational during severe flooding.
