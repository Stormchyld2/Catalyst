# Location Summary

Old Quay is Lumen's oldest continuously occupied waterfront district, built around stone warehouses, narrow streets, elevated walkways, and a nineteenth-century seawall.

## Canonical Identity

## Physical Description

Dark stone, salt-streaked brick, narrow lanes, steel tram supports, glass-fronted renovations, and frequent reflections from harbor water.

## Geography & Layout

The district follows the original shoreline rather than the later rectilinear street grid. Several brass survey markers survive in building foundations.

## Atmosphere & Sensory Details

Salt, diesel, wet stone, gulls, tram vibration, foghorns, and reflected signage in rain.

## History

Old Quay predates modern flood barriers. Much of Meridian's base map was aligned to older survey data originating here.

## Culture & Population

Dock workers, tradespeople, long-term residents, new technology offices, and expensive converted warehouses share the district.

## Important Areas

Municipal Archives annex, Calder Street, decommissioned tide station, elevated tram viaduct.

## Connected Characters

Mara Vale lives here. Tomas works here frequently. Elias used several local maintenance sites.

## Connected Organizations

Lumen Civic Government and Helix Municipal Systems maintain infrastructure in the district.

## Story Function

Provides access to archives and surviving physical survey evidence.

## Events & Scenes

Chapter Two includes the first successful use of the Venn Survey Compass here.

## Continuity Notes

Mara lives above a closed watch-repair shop on Calder Street.
