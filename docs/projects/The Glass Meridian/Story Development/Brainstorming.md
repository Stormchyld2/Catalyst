# Brainstorming

- The city believes maps are objective, but every map is also a political decision.
- Start with one small impossibility: a residential block exists physically but not on the official map.
- The glass compass does not point north. It aligns with old survey markers.
- Flood prediction is useful and saves lives; the antagonist system should not be cartoonishly evil.
- The missing engineer discovered that model uncertainty is being simplified differently depending on redevelopment priorities.
- Nightwater created the political conditions that made Meridian indispensable.
- Mara must eventually choose between perfect certainty and timely action.
- Recurring imagery: glass, contour lines, salt residue, reflected light, erased pencil marks, tide gauges.
- Lumen should feel technologically advanced but physically old.
