# Foundation

In the storm-battered coastal city of Lumen, municipal cartographer Mara Vale discovers that the city's celebrated flood-prediction network is quietly deleting entire neighborhoods from its official maps. When missing engineer Elias Venn leaves behind a modified glass survey compass, Mara follows a physical trail connecting a civic technology contractor, the city government, and a disaster from twelve years earlier.

The central design principle is that Meridian is useful, sophisticated, and genuinely life-saving. The conflict comes from the human priorities embedded in the way uncertainty is translated into public decisions.
