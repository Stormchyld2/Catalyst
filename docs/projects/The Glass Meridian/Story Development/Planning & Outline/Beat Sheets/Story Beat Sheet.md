# Story Beat Sheet

The beat sheet follows the Master Outline and gives each scene a small set of actionable story beats. It is intentionally concise so the demo can show how planning context follows a scene into Write.

## Chapter 1 — The Missing Block

### Scene 1 — Map QA
- Mara spots the road-layer mismatch during routine QA.
- Archive comparison proves Bellwether Row existed in yesterday's map.
- No authorized change explains the deletion.

### Scene 2 — Bellwether Row
- Mara verifies the block physically exists.
- A resident challenges the authority of the mapping office.
- The tablet places Mara in an empty field.

### Scene 3 — Tomas
- Mara asks Tomas about sensor failures.
- Tomas reveals Elias left a glass survey compass.
- Elias's message establishes the ground-versus-map rule.

## Chapter 2 — The Old Meridian

### Scene 1 — Archive Layer
- Old survey sheets reveal a chain of brass markers.
- Elias's maintenance history overlaps the marker network.
- Mara realizes the compass may encode infrastructure rather than direction.

### Scene 2 — First Marker
- Mara and Tomas test the compass at Old Quay.
- The glass aligns with the brass marker.
- A second line points toward a decommissioned tide station.

### Scene 3 — Nia's Pattern
- Nia presents property acquisitions following risk reclassification.
- Mara sees Bellwether Row among the affected blocks.
- The mapping anomaly gains a financial consequence.

## Chapter 3 — The Tide Station

### Scene 1 — Decommissioned
- The compass leads Mara and Tomas to the old tide station.
- Official records say the station is inactive.
- Live power and telemetry prove the record is false.

### Scene 2 — Elias's Cache
- Mara finds Elias's offline maintenance cache.
- Raw uncertainty differs from the public Meridian layer.
- Elias preserved evidence outside the normal system.

### Scene 3 — Helix Notice
- Helix security arrives under valid municipal authority.
- Mara cannot establish whether the response is routine or targeted.
- She leaves with only part of Elias's cache.

## Chapter 4 — The Nightwater File

### Scene 1 — Nia's Archive
- Nia reconstructs the Nightwater public record.
- Mara finds references to a secondary surge forecast.
- Officials treated the forecast as too uncertain for evacuation action.

### Scene 2 — East Reach
- Mara traces the historical flood line through East Reach.
- Present-day classification changes overlap past losses.
- The abstract model decision becomes physically visible.

### Scene 3 — The Decision Log
- Elias's index leads Mara to a surviving decision log.
- The model's warning was not absent; it was simplified away.
- Mara identifies policy judgment embedded in the technical workflow.

## Chapter 5 — The Suppressed Layer

### Scene 1 — Raw Meridian
- Mara obtains temporary raw-layer access.
- She compares uncertainty against the public risk display.
- Redevelopment zones show a repeatable smoothing pattern.

### Scene 2 — Acquisition Map
- Nia overlays acquisition records.
- Classification shifts precede acquisition clusters.
- Correlation strengthens the investigation without yet proving individual motive.

### Scene 3 — Storm Watch
- A severe storm enters the forecast window.
- Mara realizes disclosure could damage evacuation trust.
- The investigation acquires a deadline.

## Chapter 6 — Two Maps

### Scene 1 — Independent Layer
- The team combines raw telemetry with old survey control points.
- Tomas verifies physical markers while Mara rebuilds the map.
- The independent layer becomes credible enough to publish.

### Scene 2 — Elias Returns
- Elias contacts Mara after recognizing his audit trail.
- He explains why he abandoned formal reporting channels.
- His evidence confirms the mechanism but does not replace Mara's conclusions.

### Scene 3 — Publication Choice
- The group debates replacing the official map.
- Mara rejects another single authoritative simplification.
- They publish the official routes together with the uncertainty layer.

## Chapter 7 — High Water

### Scene 1 — Evacuation
- The storm reaches Lumen.
- Residents receive official routes plus uncertainty information.
- Local decisions adapt around risks the simplified layer obscured.

### Scene 2 — Bellwether Row
- Mara returns to the first missing block.
- Residents act on information they previously lacked.
- Bellwether Row survives the worst surge.

### Scene 3 — After the Water
- The city begins a public Meridian audit.
- Helix loses its exclusive contract.
- Mara returns to the archive committed to accountable mapping.
