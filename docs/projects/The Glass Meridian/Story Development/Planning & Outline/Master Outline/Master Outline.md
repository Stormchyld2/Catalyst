# Master Outline

This outline carries The Glass Meridian beyond the two drafted demo chapters so LithoMark can demonstrate drafted, planned, and next-action manuscript views.

## Chapter 1 — The Missing Block

### Scene 1 — Map QA

Mara performs a routine review of a municipal zoning update and notices Bellwether Row is absent from the current public Meridian layer.

**Purpose:** Introduce Mara, Meridian, and the anomaly.  
**POV:** Mara  
**Location:** Meridian Operations Center  
**Reveal:** Physical parcel records and the public map disagree.

### Scene 2 — Bellwether Row

Mara visits East Reach expecting a demolition site and finds an occupied residential block.

**Purpose:** Make the data anomaly tangible.  
**POV:** Mara  
**Location:** East Reach  
**Reveal:** Residents have received contradictory notices about their official risk status.

### Scene 3 — Tomas

Mara asks Tomas whether local sensors have been malfunctioning. He gives her the glass compass Elias left with him.

**Purpose:** Reconnect the siblings and introduce Elias's physical clue.  
**POV:** Mara  
**Location:** Tomas's workshop  
**Reveal:** Elias told Tomas, "If the map stops matching the ground, use the ground."

## Chapter 2 — The Old Meridian

### Scene 1 — Archive Layer

Mara compares pre-Meridian survey sheets with the modern base layer.

**Purpose:** Establish the old brass survey network.  
**POV:** Mara  
**Location:** Municipal Archives  
**Reveal:** Several old marker IDs correspond to recent maintenance locations visited by Elias.

### Scene 2 — First Marker

Mara and Tomas test the compass at an Old Quay survey marker.

**Purpose:** Demonstrate the object's real mechanism.  
**POV:** Mara  
**Location:** Old Quay  
**Reveal:** The compass aligns to another marker near a decommissioned tide station.

### Scene 3 — Nia's Pattern

Nia confronts Mara with property records showing accelerated acquisitions in areas recently reclassified by Meridian.

**Purpose:** Merge civic-data and financial threads.  
**POV:** Mara  
**Location:** Old Quay café  
**Reveal:** Bellwether Row is one of several affected blocks.

## Chapter 3 — The Tide Station

### Scene 1 — Decommissioned

Mara and Tomas follow the compass to a tide station officially removed from Meridian's active infrastructure inventory.

**Purpose:** Move the investigation from mapping discrepancies into hidden physical infrastructure.  
**POV:** Mara  
**Location:** Old Quay tide station  
**Reveal:** The station is still receiving power and recording raw tidal telemetry.

### Scene 2 — Elias's Cache

Inside the station, Mara finds an offline maintenance cache left by Elias.

**Purpose:** Give Mara evidence that Elias deliberately preserved data outside Meridian.  
**POV:** Mara  
**Location:** Old Quay tide station  
**Reveal:** Elias was comparing raw sensor uncertainty with the simplified public risk layer.

### Scene 3 — Helix Notice

A Helix security team arrives with a legitimate municipal access order, forcing Mara and Tomas to leave before they can copy the entire cache.

**Purpose:** Put institutional pressure on the investigation without turning Helix into a cartoon villain.  
**POV:** Mara  
**Location:** Old Quay tide station  
**Reveal:** Someone inside the civic system knows the old station is still active.

## Chapter 4 — The Nightwater File

### Scene 1 — Nia's Archive

Nia brings Mara archived reporting and public-hearing records from the Nightwater Flood.

**Purpose:** Connect the present anomaly to the city's defining disaster.  
**POV:** Mara  
**Location:** Nia's newsroom archive  
**Reveal:** A secondary surge forecast existed before Nightwater but was treated as too uncertain to drive evacuation.

### Scene 2 — East Reach

Mara walks the Nightwater inundation line through East Reach and compares it with today's Meridian classifications.

**Purpose:** Make the historical consequences physical and personal.  
**POV:** Mara  
**Location:** East Reach  
**Reveal:** Several neighborhoods now being downgraded in the public map overlap the areas that bore Nightwater's worst losses.

### Scene 3 — The Decision Log

Using Elias's cache index, Mara locates a surviving decision log showing how uncertainty was simplified during Nightwater.

**Purpose:** Shift the mystery from faulty technology to policy embedded in technology.  
**POV:** Mara  
**Location:** Meridian Operations Center  
**Reveal:** The model identified the danger; officials chose which uncertainty the public would see.

## Chapter 5 — The Suppressed Layer

### Scene 1 — Raw Meridian

Mara gains temporary access to Meridian's raw uncertainty layer and compares it with the public map.

**Purpose:** Prove the current system still transforms uncertainty before publication.  
**POV:** Mara  
**Location:** Meridian Operations Center  
**Reveal:** The public layer systematically smooths risk differently across redevelopment zones.

### Scene 2 — Acquisition Map

Nia overlays property acquisitions on Mara's comparison.

**Purpose:** Bring the civic-data and financial threads together.  
**POV:** Mara  
**Location:** Old Quay café  
**Reveal:** The strongest classification shifts precede clusters of acquisition offers.

### Scene 3 — Storm Watch

A severe storm enters the forecast window while Mara is still trying to determine who authorized the optimization rules.

**Purpose:** Turn the investigation into an immediate civic dilemma.  
**POV:** Mara  
**Location:** Meridian Operations Center  
**Reveal:** Exposing Meridian now could undermine evacuation trust at exactly the moment the city needs it.

## Chapter 6 — Two Maps

### Scene 1 — Independent Layer

Mara, Tomas, and Nia begin assembling an independent map from raw telemetry, old survey markers, and archived elevation data.

**Purpose:** Convert investigation into action.  
**POV:** Mara  
**Location:** Tomas's workshop  
**Reveal:** The old physical network can validate enough of the raw data to make the suppressed uncertainty legible.

### Scene 2 — Elias Returns

Elias makes contact after learning Mara has reconstructed his audit trail.

**Purpose:** Resolve the missing-engineer thread while preserving Mara as the person who solved the core problem.  
**POV:** Mara  
**Location:** East Reach  
**Reveal:** Elias disappeared because he believed formal reporting channels had been compromised, not because Meridian itself was fabricated.

### Scene 3 — Publication Choice

The group decides to release the official evacuation map alongside the uncertainty layer rather than replacing one simplified map with another.

**Purpose:** Force Mara's thematic choice between perfect certainty and accountable uncertainty.  
**POV:** Mara  
**Location:** Meridian Operations Center  
**Reveal:** Public trust can be served by showing limits instead of pretending they do not exist.

## Chapter 7 — High Water

### Scene 1 — Evacuation

The storm arrives and Lumen evacuates under both the official routes and the newly visible uncertainty warnings.

**Purpose:** Test the characters' decisions under real conditions.  
**POV:** Mara  
**Location:** Lumen / East Reach  
**Reveal:** Residents use the added information to make local choices the simplified map could not anticipate.

### Scene 2 — Bellwether Row

Mara returns to Bellwether Row during the surge response.

**Purpose:** Bring the story back to the place where the anomaly became real.  
**POV:** Mara  
**Location:** Bellwether Row  
**Reveal:** The block survives the worst surge because residents were finally included in the information flow.

### Scene 3 — After the Water

In the aftermath, Helix loses its exclusive contract and Lumen begins a public audit of Meridian.

**Purpose:** Resolve the institutional conflict without claiming the city can eliminate uncertainty.  
**POV:** Mara  
**Location:** Municipal Archives  
**Reveal:** Mara returns to mapping with a new standard: maps cannot be neutral, but they can be accountable.
