# Style & Voice

## Point of View

Third person limited, primarily Mara Vale.

## Tense

Past tense.

## Tone

Grounded, atmospheric, investigative, and restrained.

## Prose Priorities

Favor concrete sensory information, infrastructure, maps, and visible consequences. Technical language should remain understandable in context.

## Character Lens

Mara instinctively notices alignment, scale, distance, missing layers, repeated patterns, and errors in representation.
