# Synopsis

Mara Vale is a municipal cartographer in Lumen, a coastal city whose survival depends on the Meridian System, a network that predicts storm surge and automatically updates evacuation maps. While correcting a routine zoning layer, Mara finds a contradiction: Bellwether Row exists in the physical city but has vanished from every official digital map produced during the last six months.

Her search leads to Elias Venn, an infrastructure engineer who disappeared after attempting to audit Meridian. Before vanishing, Elias left a glass survey compass with Mara's estranged brother, Tomas. The compass points not north but toward obsolete brass survey markers buried throughout the oldest parts of the city.

Mara's investigation brings her into conflict with the Lumen Office of Civic Resilience and Helix Municipal Systems, the private contractor that maintains Meridian. She learns that Meridian's public flood-risk maps are not simply predicting danger. An optimization layer changes how uncertainty is displayed, making some districts appear more dangerous and others more secure. Those maps influence insurance, redevelopment, evacuation investment, and property acquisition.

The trail reaches back to the Nightwater Flood twelve years earlier, when a failed evacuation killed hundreds in East Reach. An experimental forecasting model had correctly identified a secondary surge path, but officials declined to act because the forecast was uncertain and would have required diverting resources from the financial district.

As another severe storm approaches, Mara discovers that the same decision logic remains embedded in Meridian. Exposing the system immediately could destroy public confidence during an active evacuation. Remaining silent could repeat Nightwater.

Mara, Tomas, journalist Nia Corren, and a reluctant city analyst assemble an independent map using old survey markers and raw tidal telemetry. They release both the official evacuation map and the suppressed uncertainty layer, giving residents information the simplified map withheld.

The storm damages Lumen but does not repeat the Nightwater catastrophe. Helix loses its exclusive contract, several officials resign, and the city begins rebuilding Meridian under public oversight. Mara returns to the archive knowing maps cannot be neutral, but they can be accountable.
