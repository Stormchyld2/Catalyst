# Themes & Core Ideas

## Visibility

Who is shown, who is omitted, and who has the authority to define what counts as visible.

## Trust

The story distinguishes trust from obedience. Institutions can be necessary without being beyond scrutiny.

## Uncertainty

Characters repeatedly confront the pressure to turn uncertain information into simple answers.

## Accountability

Responsibility remains even when a harmful result emerges from many individually reasonable decisions.
