# Event Summary

A rapidly intensifying coastal storm produced two overlapping surge paths. Official evacuation planning anticipated the primary harbor surge but failed to act on an experimental model predicting a secondary eastern surge.

## Canonical Date & Place

October 14, 2026. Lumen waterfront and East Reach.

## Participants

Residents, emergency services, city leadership, transit operators, and early resilience-modeling consultants.

## What Happened

The primary evacuation plan directed resources toward central harbor districts. East Reach received a late warning after secondary drainage channels reversed.

## Causes

Severe weather, aging flood infrastructure, fragmented data, and a decision not to act on a low-confidence secondary forecast.

## Immediate Consequences

Hundreds killed, major infrastructure loss, widespread displacement.

## Long-Term Consequences

Creation of the Office of Civic Resilience, construction of new barriers, rapid adoption of Meridian, and political commitment to one authoritative map.

## Character Impact

Tomas and Lena Vale were trapped in East Reach. Mara was away at university and carries guilt about not being present.

## Evidence / Sources

Municipal inquiry, transit logs, archived tide-gauge records, and a suppressed model memorandum later found by Elias.

## Story Function

Explains both the city's dependence on Meridian and the institutional fear of presenting uncertainty.

## Continuity Notes

Nightwater occurred twelve years before the main story in 2038.
