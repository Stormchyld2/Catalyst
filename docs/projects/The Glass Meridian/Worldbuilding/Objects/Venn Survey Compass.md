# Object Summary

A palm-sized brass-and-glass survey compass with no conventional magnetic needle.

## Canonical Identity

A modified retired municipal optical survey instrument.

## Physical Description

Circular brass body, beveled glass face, etched meridian lines, a small mirrored prism, and a worn leather case.

## Origin & History

Built from a retired municipal survey instrument. Elias modified it to align optically with coded brass markers installed throughout Lumen.

## Ownership & Custody

Elias Venn → Tomas Vale → Mara Vale.

## Capabilities / Properties

When held over a surviving survey marker, the glass prism reveals an etched alignment line indicating the next marker in the old network.

It is mechanical and optical, not supernatural.

## Symbolic Meaning

A physical reference system outside the digital infrastructure.

## Story Function

Allows Mara to reconstruct a chain of locations Elias used to hide evidence.

## Appearances & Events

Introduced in Chapter One. First successfully used in Chapter Two.

## Continuity Notes

The compass never points north and should not be described as magnetic.
