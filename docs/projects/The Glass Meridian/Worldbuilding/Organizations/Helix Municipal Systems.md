# Organization Summary

Helix Municipal Systems develops decision-support software for municipal infrastructure, emergency planning, and climate adaptation.

## Canonical Identity

Helix is a private contractor, not a city agency.

## Purpose & Mission

Public mission: turn complex infrastructure data into actionable civic decisions.

## History

Helix entered Lumen after Nightwater and rapidly became embedded in the rebuilding program.

## Structure

Regional engineering, modeling, field maintenance, government relations, and data operations teams.

## Leadership

Celia Rhun, regional managing director.

## Membership

Engineers, modelers, field technicians, product staff, government-relations specialists.

## Resources & Influence

Controls proprietary components of Meridian, holds long-term municipal contracts, and employs many former city technical staff.

## Culture & Internal Politics

Engineering staff generally believe the system saves lives. Senior leadership prioritizes public confidence, continuity, and contract stability.

## Allies & Rivals

Primary institutional partner: Lumen Civic Government. Critics include East Reach neighborhood coalitions and investigative journalists.

## Story Function

Institutional antagonist without being uniformly malicious.

## Key Events

Post-Nightwater contract award, Meridian expansion, Elias Venn's internal audit.

## Continuity Notes

Elias Venn is a Helix employee, not a city employee.
