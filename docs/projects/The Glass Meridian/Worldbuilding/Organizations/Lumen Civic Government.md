# Government Summary

Lumen is governed by an elected mayor and council with substantial authority delegated to professional municipal agencies.

## Canonical Identity

Municipal government of the city of Lumen.

## Territory & Population

The city covers the central ridge, harbor districts, eastern floodplain, and outer barrier islands.

## History

Nightwater reshaped the government around resilience, emergency planning, and infrastructure modernization.

## Constitutional Structure

Executive mayor, district council, independent municipal auditor, and charter agencies.

## Leadership

Mayor Ansel Dorr.

## Institutions

**Office of Civic Resilience:** Emergency mapping and evacuation planning.  
**Harbor Authority:** Tide gauges and marine infrastructure.  
**Transit Bureau:** Elevated tram evacuation routes.  
**Municipal Archives:** Historical maps and survey records.

## Laws & Justice

Emergency orders permit temporary closure and evacuation designations based on official risk maps.

## Economy & Resources

Port revenue, municipal bonds, resilience grants, and redevelopment partnerships fund major infrastructure.

## Military & Security

No military role. Municipal emergency services and police support evacuations and critical infrastructure security.

## Political Culture

Highly technocratic after Nightwater. Public trust is tied closely to the belief that Lumen learned from the disaster.

## Factions & Opposition

East Reach neighborhood coalitions argue that resilience policy is being used to justify redevelopment and managed retreat.

## Foreign Relations

Regional coordination with neighboring coastal municipalities and national weather authorities.

## Story Function

The government is both dependent on Meridian and responsible for scrutinizing it.

## Continuity Notes

No single official should be treated as controlling all of Meridian.
